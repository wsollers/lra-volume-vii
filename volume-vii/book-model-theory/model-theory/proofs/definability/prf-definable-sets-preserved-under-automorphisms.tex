\begin{proposition*}[Definable Sets Are Preserved Under Automorphisms]
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an $\mathcal{L}$-structure. If
$S\subseteq A^n$ is definable and $\pi$ is an automorphism of $\mathcal{A}$,
then $\pi(S)=S$.
\end{proposition*}
